# 18-Day Meta Ads Strategy: The Hybrid Authority Sprint (Synthesized 4-Expert Plan)

This strategy is built for a 18-day profitability window, leveraging the physical gym as a trust engine for high-ticket online coaching.

## 1. THE STRATEGIC OFFER
**Name:** The [Gym Name] 28-Day Rapid Transformation Challenge.
**Core Value:** Physical gym authority meets digital convenience ("Hybrid Results").
**Deliverables:** 
- **Trainerize Workouts:** Personalized programming from a real gym owner.
- **Weekly Accountability Audits:** 1-on-1 video or voice check-ins (The "Secret Sauce").
- **Private Community Access:** Peer support and motivation.
- **Nutrition Blueprint:** The "Gym-Tested" metabolic reset plan.
**Price:** $497 - $597 (Paid In Full - Front End).
**The Backend:** Transition to $197/mo maintenance program after 28 days.

## 2. THE RISK REVERSAL (The Guarantee)
**The Conditional Performance Guarantee:** 
> "If you follow the protocol, log your workouts in Trainerize, and hit your macros for 28 days and don't see a visible change in your physique, we will work with you for FREE until you do."

## 3. AD CREATIVES & SCRIPTS
### Creative A: The "Authority Walkthrough" (Video)
- **Visuals:** You walking through your physical facility, showing members training, and holding your phone with the Trainerize app.
- **Hook (0-5s):** "Stop following generic PDF workouts from 'coaches' who don't even own a barbell. If you want real results, you need a system tested in a real gym."
- **Body:** Explain the Hybrid Protocol. You get the elite programming from the gym + the 24/7 accountability in Trainerize.
- **CTA:** "I'm looking for 10 people this month. Click 'Learn More' to see if you qualify."

### Creative B: The "Social Proof Static"
- **Visual:** High-contrast "Before & After" split image of a real gym member.
- **Headline:** From [Starting Point] to [Result] in 28 Days.
- **Body Copy:** Simple and punchy. Emphasize that this was achieved through the [Gym Name] Method.

## 4. META CAMPAIGN SETUP (Post-Andromeda / GEM)
- **Objective:** Leads (Using Meta's Instant Forms / Lead Forms).
- **Format:** CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization) at $50/day minimum.
- **Targeting:** 
  - **Ad Set 1:** Broad (Age 25-50, Gender, Location: National/Global, NO interests).
  - **Ad Set 2:** Competitors (Interests like Peloton, Equinox, CrossFit).
- **Optimization:** Advantage+ Placements (Let the AI find the cheapest lead sources).

## 5. THE 18-DAY PROFITABILITY ROADMAP
- **Day 1-3 (Setup):** Record the Authority Video and gather social proof assets.
- **Day 4 (Launch):** Launch Lead Form campaign. Use "Higher Intent" settings on the form (add a qualifying question).
- **Day 5-18 (Execution):** 
  - **Speed to Lead:** Call or text every lead within **5 minutes**. 
  - **The Close:** Book "Clarity Calls" to sell the $497 - $597 package.
  - **Optimization:** If CPL (Cost per Lead) is > $12 after 48 hours, change the hook. If Lead-to-Booked Call rate is < 20%, add a qualifying question.

## 6. MATH TO PROFIT (Hormozi Framework)
- **Daily Ad Spend:** $50 ($900 over 18 days).
- **Target CPL:** $10 = 90 leads.
- **Booked Calls (20%):** 18 calls.
- **Close Rate (25%):** 4.5 sales.
- **Total Revenue:** 4.5 sales x $500 = $2,250.
- **Net Profit:** $1,350 (plus 4 long-term recurring clients).
