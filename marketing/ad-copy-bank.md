# Online Coaching Ad Copy Bank - Static Ads

## Strategy: The "Ugly" PSA Format
High-contrast, bold text, no lifestyle imagery. 

### Hook: Authority (The 200+ Proof)
- Headline: ATTENTION
- Sub-headline: 200+ TRANSFORMATIONS COMPLETED
- Offer: I’M GIVING AWAY 5 CUSTOM "FAT-LOSS BLUEPRINTS" THIS WEEK
- Urgency: 6 WEEKS TO A NEW YOU
- CTA: GET YOUR FREE PLAN TODAY

### Hook: Pain Point (The Plateau)
- Headline: STUCK AT THE SAME WEIGHT?
- Sub-headline: STOP GUESSING IN THE GYM
- Offer: GET A FREE METABOLIC AUDIT & CUSTOM SETUP
- Transition: If you like the plan, we work together. If not, keep it for free.
- CTA: START YOUR TRANSFORMATION TODAY

### Hook: Identity (Busy Professionals)
- Headline: ATTENTION: BUSY PROFESSIONALS
- Sub-headline: TIRED OF FEELING SLUGGISH & SOFT?
- Offer: FREE 6-WEEK NUTRITION & TRAINING ROADMAP
- Value: BUILT FOR YOUR SCHEDULE (AT NO COST)
- CTA: CLAIM YOUR BLUEPRINT BELOW

## The "Trojan Horse" Script for the Call:
1. Deliver the Blueprint/Audit first (Value).
2. Ask: "Would you like me to help you implement this so you actually get the result?"
3. Transition to the coaching offer.
