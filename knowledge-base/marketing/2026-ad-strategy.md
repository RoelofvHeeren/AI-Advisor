## 2. Creative Strategy (Meta Advisor & Copywriting)
**Rule:** Creative is the targeting. Use broad/advantage+ targeting and let the script find the audience.

### Video Script Script 1: "The Metabolism Hook" (Reel/Square Format)
* **Hook (0-3s):** "Stop doing cardio if you’re trying to lose the 'office gut' before summer."
* **Retain (3-15s):** "Most [City Name] professionals fail because they starve themselves all day then binge at 8 PM. Your metabolism isn't broken; your strategy is."
* **Reward (15-45s):** "That’s why we built the Metabolic Reset. We use 30-minute strength sessions and a 'Macro-Matching' meal plan so you can eat steak and still see your abs."
* **Proof:** Flash 3-5 transformation photos FAST.
* **CTA:** "Click 'Learn More' and fill out the form. I’m only looking for 10 people this month. Do it now."

### Creative Guidelines:
* **Style:** Lo-fi, iPhone-shot, walk-and-talk or sitting-in-the-gym.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (Reels) and 4:5 (Feed).
* **Subtitles:** Use captions for the first 5 seconds to catch attention on mute.

## 3. Meta Ad Setup (2025/2026 Andromeda Best Practices)
* **Campaign Type:** Sales (if website) or Leads (if Instant Forms).
* **Budget:** $30-$50/day min per gym location.
* **Targeting:** 
    * Advantage+ Audience (Broad).
    * Location: 10-15 mile radius around gym.
    * Age: 30-55.
    * Interests: None (Let the creative target).
* **Lead Form Settings:**
    * Use **"Higher Intent"** Lead Forms.
    * **Custom Question:** "On a scale of 1-10, how committed are you to changing your health in the next 6 weeks?"
    * **Review Step:** Enable the "Slide to Confirm" feature.

## 4. Sales/Follow-up (GHL Integration)
* **Speed to Lead:** Leads must be called/texted within **5 minutes**.
* **Initial SMS:** "Hey [Name]! This is [Coach Name] from [Gym]. I just saw you applied for our 42-Day Reset. Do you have 2 mins for a quick call to see if you're a fit?"
* **Lead Scoring:** Prioritize leads who answered "10" on the commitment question.
