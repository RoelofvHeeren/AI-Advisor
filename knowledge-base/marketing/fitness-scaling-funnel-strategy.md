## When and Why Use Paid Ads?
Whatever you're advertising, you're advertising to that account. If you're advertising and promoting through your Instagram or your Facebook, whoever's clicking on the links they're going to land back on your page and your account. It's really important you understand this: if I'm running ads through my Instagram account or Facebook account and people land up back on my page and it's absolutely garbage—no results, poor quality content—you're going to waste a lot of money because people are not going to see credibility. Get the fundamentals of your organic right first.

## Objectives
Inside the business meta Suite (Business Manager), you choose an objective. Each objective has a specific outcome:

1. **Awareness and Traffic**: Getting more eyeballs, video views, and brand awareness. Good for building a better audience and increasing following.
2. **Lead Generation**: Getting an email, phone number, or DM. Building an email database using low-level contacts who want a free guide, cheat sheet, etc.
3. **Engagement**: Getting people to take action such as sending DMs. Works well with tools like ManyChat to start automated conversations that lead to booked calls.
4. **New Sales**: Higher value, direct application forms or product sales. Facebook charges more for these because the value return is higher.

## Boosting vs. Business Suite
Boosting is simple and works well for gaining followers and starting DM conversations. You can start with as little as £10 a day. However, for better targeting and specific marketing outcomes, you should use the Meta Business Suite.

## The Strategy
Start by focusing on the organic content first. Once you have a stagnating growth or want to increase your income (e.g., going from 25k to 50k a month), add paid advertising as an additional marketing tool. Use the "Value Ladder": awareness -> engagement -> leads -> applications -> sales.

# The ULTIMATE Funnel for Scaling Online Fitness Coaching to $50k/month

If you're an online health and fitness coach and you want to scale your business to $50,000 a month plus, I'm going to break down the best strategy to get there.

The traditional way of posting content and sending DMs will only get you so far. It's hard to scale when you're overwhelmed by hundreds of manual conversations. Once you're doing $20k-$30k a month, it's time to build systems.

## The Problem with Manual DMs
Manual follow-up is chaos at scale. Automations (like ManyChat) are often limited by 24-hour windows. You end up needing a massive team just to organize the chaos.

## The Solution: The Value-Driven VSL Funnel
The goal is to have one incredible resource (a VSL or Video Sales Letter) that builds enough trust for people to book themselves. This must be paired with a strong personal brand.

### The Funnel Steps:
1. **Ad/Social Content**: Drives traffic to an opt-in page.
2. **Opt-in Page**: Promises a specific result (How to [Goal] in [Time] without [Pain]).
3. **Training Page (VSL)**: A 20-30 minute video that solves their problem, tells your story, provides a roadmap, and offers a call.
4. **Automations**: Sequential SMS and Email follow-ups to get people to watch the video or book a call.
5. **Schedule/Application**: A calendar (or application form if your calendar is full) to book the consultation.
6. **Thank You Page**: A short video confirming the call and setting expectations.

### The Numbers:
- **Conversion**: Aim for 10% of VSL viewers to book a call.
- **Cost per Call**: Target under $150.
- **Cost per Client**: Target under $500 (or 20-25% of your offer price).
- **Offer Price**: Ideally a $2,000+ program to ensure profitability.

## Profitability Strategy
To cover ad costs, consider a higher initial deposit or "setup fee" (e.g., $500-$1,000) followed by a monthly recurring fee (e.g., $350-$400). This balances immediate cash flow with long-term stability.

# Best Instagram Ad Strategy for Online Fitness Coaches

## The Foundation
If you have a good product that you've sold before, you are ready for ads. You must help one specific person (niche specific). If your message attracts everybody, it attracts nobody.

## Platform Choice
Instagram is predominantly the best platform right now for fitness coaches. It serves as a social media business card where people vet you after seeing an ad.

## Creative Diversity
Meta's AI (Andromeda) thrives on diversification. Mix your creatives:
- **Videos** (Talking to camera)
- **Statics** (Images with text)
- **B-roll** (Atmospheric footage)
People consume content differently, so give the platform options to find what works for each user.

## Likes vs. Money
Don't equate likes to dollars. Viral content often attracts the wrong people. Focus on specificity to attract the right "Asian desk worker" or "rugby player" who is actually willing to pay.

## Success Metrics
A client went from $5k/month to $600k/month in 6 months using these scaling strategies. The goal is to turn $1 into $10+ through predictable advertising.
