# How to Use Instagram Ads for Your Online Coaching Business

This video covers the full strategy of using Instagram ads to scale a fitness coaching business.

## The "Social Media Business Card"
Instagram is unique because your profile acts as your portfolio. When people see an ad, they click your profile. If it's empty or unimpressive, the ad fails. Maintain high-quality organic content to build trust instantly.

## Ad Funnel Basics
- **Cold Traffic**: People who don't know you. Focus on "Hook, Retain, Reward." Give a quick win or a free resource.
- **Retargeting**: People who visited your profile or website but didn't book. Show them testimonials and "day in the life" content to humanize the brand.

## Ad Creative Hooks
Common hooks that work for fitness:
1. "The [Specific Group] Guide to [Result]"
2. "Why [Popular Method] is failing you (and what to do instead)"
3. "How I helped [Client Name] lose [Weight] in [Time]"

# How to run ads for your online fitness business | Intro to paid ads

## Getting Started
Don't jump into ads if you haven't sold your program organically. You need to know your "avatar" and their "pain points" first.

## The Budget
Start with $10-$20 a day. This is "data collection" money. You aren't buying clients yet; you're buying information on what headlines and images people click on.

## Simple Lead Magnet
The easiest way to start is a "Lead Magnet" (PDF, Checklist, or short video).
1. Person sees ad.
2. Person clicks and lands on a simple page (Name/Email).
3. Person gets the guide.
4. You follow up via email/SMS to book a call.

## Consistency is Key
Don't turn ads off after 3 days because you didn't get a $2,000 client. Give the algorithm time to learn.
