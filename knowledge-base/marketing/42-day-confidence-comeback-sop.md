# SOP: THE 42-DAY CONFIDENCE COMEBACK CAMPAIGN

## 1. THE STRATEGIC SHIFT (Consensus)
We are moving away from the "Metabolic Reset" (too scientific/jargon-heavy) and the "6-Week Challenge" (too commoditized). 
**The Goal:** Position the gym as the solution to "Confidence" and "Fitting back into clothes," not just "Working out."

---

## 2. THE GRAND SLAM OFFER (Hormozi Framework)
*   **The Name:** The 42-Day Confidence Comeback.
*   **The Dream Outcome:** Feeling like yourself again and fitting into "goal clothes" (the jeans in the back of the closet).
*   **The Value Stack:**
    *   42 Days of Hand-Held Coaching (High Accountability).
    *   The "Simple-Eats" Blueprint (Zero-thought nutrition).
    *   The "Social Survival Guide" (How to eat out/drink wine and still lose weight).
    *   Weekly "Win" Check-ins.
*   **The Risk Reversal (The "Jeans Test"):** 
    > "If you don’t lose at least 10lbs or fit back into your favorite pair of jeans in 42 days, we will work with you for FREE until you do."

---

## 3. THE CREATIVE: "LO-FI" AD SCRIPT
**Format:** Vertical iPhone Video. Film this on the gym floor.

*   **HOOK (0-3s):** "Hey [City]! I’m looking for 12 people who are tired of 'starting over' every single Monday."
*   **THE PROBLEM (3-15s):** "If you’ve been 'eating clean' and hitting the treadmill but your clothes still feel tight, it's not you—it's your plan. Most gyms give you a workout; we give you a result."
*   **THE SOLUTION (15-30s):** "We’re launching the 42-Day Confidence Comeback. It’s a roadmap to lose 10-15lbs without giving up your Friday night pizza or living in the gym."
*   **THE GUARANTEE (30-45s):** "The best part? If you don’t feel significantly more confident and see results in 6 weeks, you don't pay a cent. We take all the risk."
*   **CTA (45-60s):** "Tap the link below to see if we have one of the 12 spots left for this month."

---

## 4. TECHNICAL META SETTINGS (Post-Andromeda)
*   **Objective:** Lead Generation (Instant Forms).
*   **Targeting:** **BROAD.** 
    *   Radius: 10-15 miles around the gym.
    *   Age: 30-55.
    *   Interests: **LEAVE BLANK.** Let the script find the audience.
*   **Lead Form:** Use "Higher Intent" settings.
*   **Custom Question:** "What is the #1 thing you want to change about your health in the next 6 weeks?"

---

## 5. SPEED-TO-LEAD (GHL Protocol)
*   **The 5-Minute Rule:** Leads must be contacted within 5 minutes.
*   **Initial SMS:** "Hey [Name]! This is Coach [Name] from [Gym]. I just saw your application for the 42-Day Comeback. Do you have 2 mins for a quick chat to see if we're a fit?"
