# New UI for Email creation via Bulk action in Contacts

The redesigned Bulk Email Builder inside HighLevel’s Contacts screen provides a modern, unified experience for creating and sending bulk emails. With built-in pre-send contact filtering, a streamlined design workflow, and powerful tracking tools, you can confidently send personalized messages at scale — without technical complexity.

## **What is the Bulk Email Builder?**

  

The Bulk Email Builder is the enhanced email creation interface that appears when you select **Send Email** within the **Contacts → Smart Lists** section. It unifies all three of HighLevel’s email editing options — **Quick Compose**, **Smart Builder**, and **Template Selection** — into one seamless flow.

  

This experience simplifies how you design, personalize, and deliver your email campaigns while ensuring invalid or unsubscribed contacts are filtered out automatically for compliance and deliverability.

* * *

## **Dynamic Custom Object Labels (New)**

  

The Bulk Email Builder now uses **dynamic custom object labels** instead of hardcoded “Contact” or “Contacts” text. This ensures the Bulk Email modal automatically reflects the object label configured in your account, such as **Leads**, **Clients**, or another custom object.  
  

### **Why this matters**  
  

-   Keeps the Bulk Email experience consistent with your CRM configuration  
      
    
-   Supports accounts that rename or replace the default Contact object  
      
    
-   Improves clarity when working with custom objects  
      
    
-   No setup or action required — labels update automatically
    

  

### **Where you’ll see this**  
  

-   In the **Bulk Email modal header and helper text** when sending emails via:  
      
    
    **Contacts → Smart Lists → Bulk Actions → Send Email**
    

  

> This update only affects displayed labels. Bulk Email functionality, recipient selection, filtering, and delivery behavior remain unchanged.

## **Key Benefits of the New Bulk Email Builder**

  

The new Bulk Email Builder introduces several enhancements designed to improve usability, accuracy, and performance monitoring.  
  

-   **Unified workflow:** All email creation options (Quick Compose, Smart Builder, Templates) are accessible in one place.  
      
    
-   **Pre-send filtering:** Automatically excludes invalid and unsubscribed contacts before sending.  
      
    
-   **Consistent experience:** All builders share similar layouts, making onboarding easier for teams.  
      
    
-   **Advanced tracking:** Enable link tracking, UTM tagging, and automation tags directly in the editor.  
      
    
-   **Reliable delivery metrics:** Access standardized reports and stats across all email sources (Contacts, Campaigns, and Workflows).  
      
    
-   **Unified composer:** Matches Campaigns and Workflows for a consistent email-building experience.

## **Pre-Send Contact Filtering**

  

Pre-send contact filtering ensures only valid recipients receive your message.

  

HighLevel automatically reviews your selected contacts and displays a summary that shows:  
  

-   Total contacts selected  
      
    
-   Filtered contacts (invalid or unsubscribed)  
      
    
-   Final recipient list
    

  

This feature helps protect your sender reputation and improves email deliverability.

## **Email Creation Modes**

  

The Bulk Email Builder offers three flexible creation modes to match your workflow and design preferences.

  

### **Quick Compose**

  

Ideal for quick announcements or transactional updates. A simple text-focused editor for fast communication.

  

### **Smart Builder**

  

A powerful builder that allows you to choose your editing experience:  
  

-   **Design Editor** (visual, drag-and-drop)

## **Additional Email Settings**

  

Advanced settings help marketers measure performance, automate actions, and enhance personalization. Expand the **Additional Settings** drawer to access:

## **Sending Test Emails and Reviewing Campaigns**

  

Before sending, you can send a test version to verify layout and content.  
  

1.  Enter a **Test Email ID**  
      
    
2.  Click **Send Test Mail**  
      
    
3.  Click **Review Campaign** to preview and confirm details before sending

## **Sending Options**

  

The Bulk Email Builder supports multiple send modes to fit your delivery needs:  
  

-   **Send All at Once:** Immediately sends to all recipients.  
      
    
-   **Send at Scheduled Time:** Schedule a specific send date and time.  
      
    
-   **Send in Drip Mode:** Gradually sends emails in batches to optimize inbox placement.

## **How to Set Up and Send Bulk Emails**  
  

1.  Navigate to **Contacts → Smart Lists**  
      
    
2.  Select the contacts or Smart List you want to email  
      
    
3.  Click **Send Email** from the Bulk Actions toolbar  
      
    
4.  Choose your creation mode:  
      
    
    -   Quick Compose  
          
        
    -   Smart Builder  
          
        
    -   Select Existing Template  
          
        
5.  Compose your email content  
      
    
6.  Review recipients in the confirmation modal  
      
    
7.  Send a test email if needed  
      
    
8.  Click **Review Campaign**  
      
    
9.  Choose a send mode and click **Send Email**

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  

**Q: Will unsubscribed or invalid contacts receive my email?**

No. The system automatically excludes unsubscribed, invalid, or DND-marked contacts.

  

**Q: Can I use my existing templates from other areas of HighLevel?**

Yes. Templates from My Templates or the Template Library are available.

  

**Q: Can I track performance for bulk emails?**

Yes. Opens, clicks, and bounces are tracked under Bulk Actions → Show Stats.

  

**Q: Can I automate tagging based on engagement?**

Yes. Use the **Add Tags** option under Additional Settings.

  

**Q: Are Smart Lists dynamically updated for scheduled sends?**

No. Contacts are captured at scheduling time.
