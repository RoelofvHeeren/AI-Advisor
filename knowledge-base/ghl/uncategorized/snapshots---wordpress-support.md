# Snapshots - WordPress Support

WordPress Support in Snapshot lets you include complete WordPress sites (pages, themes, plugins, and settings) in Snapshots. This article explains how to select WordPress as a Snapshot asset and what to expect when you deploy the Snapshot to another sub-account.

# **What is WordPress Support in Snapshot?**

  

WordPress Support in Snapshot allows you to include one or more WordPress sites from a selected sub-account as part of a Snapshot. When that Snapshot is deployed to another sub-account, HighLevel restores the WordPress site automatically along with your other selected Snapshot assets.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of WordPress Support in Snapshot**

  

-   **Faster client launches:** Deploy a full WordPress environment without rebuilding from scratch.  
      
    
-   **Consistent delivery:** Reuse the same themes, plugins, and configuration across accounts.  
      
    
-   **Fewer setup mistakes:** Reduce missed steps by restoring the site as part of your standard Snapshot.  
      
    
-   **Repeatable processes:** Build once and roll out the same setup to new or existing sub-accounts.
    

* * *

## **How To Setup WordPress Support in a Snapshot**

  

Correctly selecting WordPress assets ensures the Snapshot restores the intended site and keeps your deployments consistent across sub-accounts.

  

### **Option A:** Create a new Snapshot with WordPress

  

1.  Log in to your **agency account**.  
      
    
2.  Go to **Account Snapshots**.  
      
    
3.  Click **Create Snapshot**.  
      
    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/LS1x1KWb7IgvoGuMisow+/Screen+Shot+2026-02-27+at+18.52.48.png)  
      
    
4.  Enter a **Snapshot Name**.  
      
    
5.  Choose the **sub-account** you want to capture data from.  
      
    
6.  Click **Next** to open the **Select Assets** modal.  
      
    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/Wx1DZVAt8B71K2mKjsNt+/Screen+Shot+2026-02-27+at+19.01.52.png)  
      
    
7.  Enable the **WordPress** checkbox.  
      
    
8.  Expand **WordPress** and select the specific site(s) you want to include.  
      
    
9.  Click on **Create.**  
      
    **![](https://jumpshare.com/share/mGhieh653e8wbCpvJWGS+/Screen+Shot+2026-02-27+at+19.05.53.png)**  
      
    

### **Option B:** Add WordPress to an existing Snapshot

  

1.  Log in to your **agency account**.  
      
    
2.  Go to **Account Snapshots**.  
      
    
3.  Find the Snapshot you want to update.  
      
    
4.  Click the **three dots (⋮)** next to the Snapshot > **Settings**.  
      
    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/ci3g6pfJy3aONsBHuXAc+/Screen+Shot+2026-02-27+at+19.07.46.png)  
      
    
5.  Scroll to the **Linked Assets** section.  
      
    
6.  Enable **WordPress**, then expand it and **choose the site(s)**.  
      
    
7.  **Save** your changes.  
      
    ![](https://jumpshare.com/share/3RDmC8u3Xo3SaNfuMpKV+/Screen+Shot+2026-02-27+at+19.09.45.png)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  

**Q: Can I include more than one WordPress site in a Snapshot?**

If multiple WordPress sites are available in the WordPress selection list, you can select the specific site(s) you want to bundle.

  

**Q: What happens to the WordPress site when I deploy the Snapshot?**

The WordPress site is restored automatically as part of the Snapshot deployment, along with your other selected assets.

  

**Q: Will this stop me from having to rebuild WordPress pages and plugins for every client?**

Yes. Including WordPress in your Snapshot allows you to reuse your WordPress environment across sub-accounts instead of starting from scratch.

  

**Q: What WordPress elements are included in a Snapshot?**

A WordPress Snapshot can include: Pages, Themes, Plugins, Site, Settings

This ensures your site structure and configuration are preserved when applied to another location.

  

**Q: Does the Snapshot include plugin and theme licenses?**

No. If your WordPress setup relies on third-party licenses or external services, you may need to re-authenticate or re-activate those items after deployment, depending on the provider.

  

**Q: Can I add WordPress to an existing Snapshot later?**

Yes. Edit the Snapshot settings and update the Linked Assets to include WordPress.
