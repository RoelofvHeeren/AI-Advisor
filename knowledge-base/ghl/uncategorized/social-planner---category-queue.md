# Social Planner - Category Queue

Looking to automate and repurpose your evergreen content with ease? HighLevel’s Category Queue feature streamlines your content strategy by allowing you to group posts by category and schedule them to publish in a recurring loop. This article walks you through how Category Queues work, how to set them up, and best practices for maximizing content reach across your platforms.

## **What’s a Category Queue in Social Planner?**

  

A category queue is a powerful way to plan your evergreen content by re-purposing the already published and future content tagged to a certain category to be re-utilised for publishing on the platforms or add new category with new content. This would allow us to not just be able to schedule a specific post, but a whole category of posts and the system would cycle through all of the posts until the end and then reset and start again.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Category Queue in Social Planner**

  

-   **Automated Evergreen Scheduling:** Set your content to cycle through a category, posting consistently without needing to manually reschedule each item.  
      
    

-   **Re-use Published & Future Content:** Easily repurpose already published posts and automatically include future content tagged to the same category.  
      
    
-   **Priority Control for New Content:** Choose to prioritize newer content so it appears earlier in the queue, keeping your feed fresh and relevant.  
      
    
-   **Custom Scheduling by Category:** Define specific days and times for each queue to ensure posts go live when your audience is most active.  
      
    
-   **Future-Proof Content Planning:** Enable future posting to seamlessly integrate new content into your ongoing publishing strategy.

## **Category Queue Public API**

  

If you want to create or manage Category Queues programmatically (for custom workflows, integrations, or automation), you can use the \*\*Category Queue Public API\*\*.

  

-   Find the API reference in \*\*Public API Docs → Social Planner → Category Queue\*\*.  
      
    
-   Authenticate with your \*\*Location API key\*\* or an \*\*OAuth token\*\*, depending on your integration type.

  

Common actions supported include:

  

-   **Fetch** available categories and queue status  
      
    
-   **Create**, list, update, and fetch category queues  
      
    
-   **Add**, update, reset, and delete queue items  
      
    
-   **View** scheduled posts in calendar formats  
      
    
-   **Delete** the current active post and schedule the next one

  

For endpoint details, request/response schemas, and examples, use the official docs:

[https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/social-planner/category-queue/index.html](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/social-planner/category-queue/index.html)

## **How to Edit a Category Queue** 

You can now edit, reorder, reschedule, and control how new posts are added to a queue, without rebuilding it from scratch.

Click on existing Category Queue and navigate to 3 dots to view editing options:  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155060861302/original/0_Bh0pGd3Mn6ZujZ9FiubHRzUCGT6W0R_g.png?1765992875)

###   

### **1\. Edit Category Queue**

You can now update the details of an existing queue without deleting or recreating it.

You can edit:

-   Post details,caption,media etc
    
-   Posting frequency (daily/weekly/etc.)
    
-   Timeslots of the Queue
    
-   Post order (drag-and-drop to rearrange)
    

###   

### **2\. Queue Preference** 

When you add a new post to the Queue, you now control where it should appear:

-   **Prioritise New Content-** **Checked:**The post is added to the top of the queue, **Unchecked:**The post is added to the end of the queue.  
      
    
-   **Enable Future Queue:** Allows all the posts with the category attached go automatically in category Queue

### **3\. View Queue Calendar**

You can now see your entire queue visually on a calendar.

Calendar Views:

-   **Weekly View**
    
-   **Monthly View**
    

You can use the calendar to:

-   See how posts are distributed
    
-   Identify gaps or content-heavy days
    

  

### **4\. Reschedule Entire Queue**

Need to shift your campaign dates?

You can now move the entire queue to start from a new date using:

-   A simple **date picker**
    
-   Smart rescheduling based on existing frequency rules
    
-   A confirmation modal before applying
    

**Example:** If your campaign is postponed by a week, simply pick a new start date and all posts will auto-adjust.

### **5\. Add Posts to Queue from Post Composer**

While creating a post, you can add it directly to a Category Queue.

**Note:** 
- Rescheduling a single post is not supported.
- Rescheduling/Reshuffle from Category Queue Calendar is not supported.

##   

### **Important Pointers**  

-   Category queues can be created for already published content tagged to a certain category. or you can create new posts and new category.
-   Category queues can be paused/ resumed at any point as we deem fit for planning the content.  
    
-   It can be deleted incase we do not want the same content to go out.  
    
-   The content of the previously published posts- can be edited/ updated while the creation of category queue.  
    
-   You can also add new post, it can be added in middle of the queue too.  
    
-   The category queue created cannot be edited, re-scheduled for a different timeframe currently.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  

**Q: How can I support future posts in the category queue?**

All the previous and future posts tagged to the 'category' will be automatically added to the category queue, incase the new posts needs to be added to front of the queue- please select the option 'Enable Future Posting' and 'Prioritise Content' .

  

**Q: Can the category queue be edited to update the scheduled date and time?**

The scheduled date and time can be planned at the category queue creation time, it cannot be edited at a later point of time.

  

**Q: How do I add new posts to the queue?**  
Go to **Queue** → **New Post**, write your content, add media, optionally create **Variations** to rotate versions, choose its position by moving the post to desired position in the queue.

  

**Q:Can I add multiple variations?**

Yes. Variations rotate/shuffle to keep your content fresh and unique.

  

**Q:What media can I use?**

Add images or videos along with text for richer posts.

  

**Q: Can I change the order later?**

Yes, open your queue to adjust the sequence of posts.
