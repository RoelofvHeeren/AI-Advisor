# Manage Comments in Social Planner

  

  

Manage comments on your social media posts directly from the social planner! The social planner supports comment management for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. This article covers how to use comment management along with the capabilities and limitations of this feature.

# **What is Comment Management?**

  

Comment Management in Social Planner helps you view and respond to comments from your posts, all in one dashboard. Instead of jumping between social media platforms, you can now manage conversations directly where you schedule and plan content. 

  

**Note**: Currently, **only comments from posts published via Social Planner are supported**. Comments on posts published natively (directly in Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn etc) will not appear here. Support for other social platforms is in progress.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of Comment Management**

  

-   **One-Stop Inbox:** View every comment alongside scheduled posts.  
      
    
-   **Real-Time Engagement:** Keep engagement consistent by liking and replying to comments.  
      
    
-   **Conversation Context:** Manage deeper discussions with threaded (nested) replies.  
      
    
-   **Ti****me-Savings:** Save time by replying without leaving Social Planner.

* * *

## **What You Can Do in Comment Management**

  

These capabilities streamline day-to-day engagement so you can respond faster, keep conversations organized, and highlight high-value interactions without leaving HighLevel.

  

  

### **Replying to Comments**

  

Respond to any top-level comment directly from Social Planner, preserving author names, emojis, and GIFs for a native feel. Fast, on-brand replies strengthen relationships and can drive additional engagements or DMs.

  

  

### **Liking Comments**

  

A quick “like” lets followers know you’ve seen and appreciated their input. Every like is recorded on Facebook and contributes to post engagement metrics, further improving reach.

  

  

### **Responding to Threaded Comments**

  

Threaded replies keep multi-person conversations organized. Click the arrow beside a parent comment to view and answer sub-threads so your replies appear precisely where the discussion is happening.

  

  

### **Smart Filters (Latest vs Top)**

  

Use the dropdown above the inbox to switch views:  
  

-   Latest: Shows the most recent activity so you never miss new questions.  
      
    
-   Top: Sorts by Facebook’s “most relevant” order, surfacing high-engagement comments first.

  

  

### **Using Mentions in Comment Replies**

  

Tag Facebook Pages or public profiles by typing “@” plus at least three characters. A list of matching public pages appears, allowing you to credit partners or acknowledge customers without leaving HighLevel.

## **Platform Capabilities Overview**

  

Comment Management inside Social Planner now supports **Facebook**, **Instagram** and **LinkedIn**, with more platforms coming soon.

  

### Facebook

-   ### 
    
    View comments on posts published via Social Planner
    
-   Reply to comments using text, emojis, and images (up to 10MB)
    
-   Threaded replies supported (multiple levels)
    
-   Like comments
    
-   Mentions supported using “@” + minimum 3 characters for search
    
-   Filter comments by Latest or Top
    
-   Emoji support available in replies
    
-   Image uploads supported (up to 10MB per Facebook limitations)
    
-   Search comments (minimum 3 characters)
    
-   Hide/Delete not supported
    
-   API support available
    
-   Media in comments (GIFs, thumbnails) visible but not clickable
    
-   Only comments from posts published via Social Planner are supported
    

  

###  Limitations:

-   Nested comment **counts** are not displayed (only top-level counts appear).
    
-   Thumbnails, images, videos, and GIFs in comments are **not clickable**.
    
-   Options to add GIFs, stickers, or avatars in replies are **not supported**.
    
-   Image uploads above **10MB** are not supported (Facebook API limit).
    
-   Search requires a **minimum of 3 characters**.
    
-   Comments on posts published **natively on Facebook** will not appear.
    
-   Hide/Delete actions are **not supported**.
    

  

  

### Instagram

-   View comments on posts published via Social Planner
    
-   Reply to comments using text, emojis, and images (up to 10MB)
    
-   Threaded replies supported
    
-   Like comments
    
-   Mentions supported only when typing the exact @username (search not available)
    
-   Filter comments by Latest or Top
    
-   Emoji support available in replies
    
-   Image uploads supported (up to 10MB per Instagram limits)
    
-   Search comments (minimum 3 characters)
    
-   Hide/Delete not supported
    
-   API support not available for Instagram comments
    
-   Media in comments visible but not clickable
    
-   Only comments from posts published via Social Planner are supported
    

  

### Limitations:

-   Mentions do **not** support search, users must type the **exact @username** (Instagram API limitation).
    
-   Thumbnails, images, videos, and GIFs in comments are **not clickable**.
    
-   Image uploads above **10MB** are not supported.
    
-   Search requires a **minimum of 3 characters**.
    
-   Hide/Delete actions are **not supported**.
    
-   API support for Instagram comment management is **limited**, which restricts advanced actions.
    
-   Comments on posts published **natively on Instagram** will not appear.
    

  

  

### Linkedin

-   View comments on LinkedIn Page posts published via Social Planner
    
-   Reply to comments using text and emojis
    
-   Support for **one level** of threaded replies
    
-   Like comments
    
-   Mention other LinkedIn users by searching for their usernames
    
-   Filter comments similar to Facebook and Instagram (Latest, Top)
    
-   Search comments (minimum 3 characters)
    
-   View all comments inside the unified inbox
    
-   Supports LinkedIn Page posts published through Social Planner
    

###   

### Limitations:

-   Only **LinkedIn Pages** are supported; personal profiles cannot be connected (API restriction)
    
-   Threaded replies are limited to **one level**
    
-   Image uploads in replies are **not supported**
    
-   Thumbnails, images, GIFs, and videos in comments are **not clickable**
    
-   Comments on posts published **natively on LinkedIn** do not appear in Social Planner
    
-   Hide/Delete comment actions are **not supported**
    
-   Search requires a minimum of **3 characters**
    
-   Replies must be within LinkedIn’s **3000-character limit**

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  

**Q: Can I see comments from other platforms?**  
Not yet. Currently, only Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn comments are supported. Other platforms are coming soon.

  

**Q: Will I see comments on posts published directly from Native Platform?**  
No. Only comments on posts published via Social Planner will appear here.

**Q: Can I delete or hide comments?**  
Not at the moment, you can only like or reply.

  

**Q: Can I reply with images and emojis?**

Yes, we support replying with emojis and images
