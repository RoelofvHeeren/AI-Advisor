#### **Overview**  
  

Welcome to the **public beta of Rentals**, our new scheduling and booking system designed for businesses that manage rentals — whether it’s cars, equipment, properties, or other bookable assets. With Rentals, you can efficiently manage availability, inventory, pricing, and multi-day bookings in one seamless platform.

**Rentals** helps you:

-   **Manage multi-day bookings** with flexible start and end dates.  
      
    
-   **Track inventory and variants** for each listing (e.g., car models, room types).  
      
    
-   **Automate pricing and discounts** for different durations, quantities, or seasons.  
      
    
-   **Prevent double-bookings** with external calendar sync.  
      
    
-   **Simplify operations** through a unified calendar and listing management experience.  
      
    

We’re excited to have you onboard as one of the first users to shape the future of this product.

## **What's New in Rentals**

  

Rentals is built to handle the unique complexities of rental-based scheduling. Here’s what’s Rentals can do:

  

  

#### **Multi-Day Booking Support**

  

-   Book assets across multiple hours, days, weeks, or months, with flexible duration and booking controls.

  

  

#### **Listings and Categories [(Help Guide)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006573)**  
  

-   Create listings for your rentals with detailed information, organized categories, and status management.

  

  

#### **Inventory & Variants [(Help Guide)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579)**

  

-   Enable quantity tracking for multiple units and define variants for different versions of the same listing.

  

  

#### **Advanced Pricing & Discounts [(Help Guide)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006611)**

  

-   Use pricing rules for seasons, days of the week, hour of day, durations, or quantities. Automatically apply discounts for long-term or bulk bookings.

  

  

#### **Calendar View [(Help Guide)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006641)**

  

-   View all bookings visually using a modern, filterable calendar that supports day, week, and month views. Identify overlaps, blocked slots, and real-time availability.

  

  

#### **External Calendar Sync**

  

-   Connect and sync bookings with third party platforms using Calendar Sync to ensure your availability is consistent across platforms.

  

  

#### **Centralized Global Settings [(Help Guide)](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006640)**

  

-   Control business operating hours, booking defaults, payment preferences, and communication templates from one location.

  

  

#### **Built-in Payments and Invoicing**

  

-   Collect payments directly from the booking flow and generate invoices automatically for each confirmed booking. Setup security deposits per your business needs.

  

  

#### **Customer Booking Page**

  

-   Offer customers a streamlined booking experience with a dedicated public-facing booking page that displays your listings, categories, pricing and availability in real-time.

## **How to Enable Rentals for a Single Sub-Account****?**

  

You can enable Rentals for sub-accounts directly from **Agency Settings**. Follow these steps to activate Rentals in a single sub-account!

  

  

#### **Step 1:** Navigate to Sub-Accounts

  

-   From the Agency dashboard, select the **Sub-Accounts** tab on the left-side navigation bar  
      
    

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066059135/original/LIF6t_S9WvKgWm9z4B7onsUBwU6DQg6CGw.jpeg?1772524420)

  

####   

#### **Step 2:** Go to Manage Client

  

-   Use the search bar or scroll to locate the sub-account where you would like to activate Rentals  
      
    
-   Click the **Three Dots (⋮)** icon in the bottom right of the sub-account panel  
      
    
-   Select the **Manage Client** Option  
      
    

#### ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066059137/original/3yPnvna8hxOV2RJsGLA9odPGiHq7_K6yvw.jpeg?1772524420)

#### **Step 3:** Turn on Rentals (Single Sub-Account)

  

-   Select **Calendar Settings** from the top navigation bar  
      
    
-   Use the toggle to Turn On **Rentals**  
      
    
-   Click the blue **Save** button to save your changes

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066085767/original/yAS_34H_1jQxSCkwiMkkeygbfgg80neKqg.png?1772538501)

  

## **How to Enable Rentals for Multiple Sub-Accounts****?**

  

You can enable Rentals for sub-accounts directly from **Agency Settings**. Follow these steps to activate Rentals for multiple sub-accounts at once!

  

  

#### **Step 1:** Navigate to Sub-Accounts

  

-   From the Agency dashboard, select the **Sub-Accounts** tab on the left-side navigation bar  
      
    

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066059136/original/eQrOEdhRotlj_Qh9JEUVUKZWxWl_cU62Lg.jpeg?1772524420)

  

  

#### **Step 2:** Open Setup Calendars

  

-   Select the sub-accounts where you would like to activate Rentals using the checkboxes  
      
    
-   Once sub-accounts are selected, click **Bulk Actions** in the upper-right corner  
      
    
-   From the drop down menu, pick **Setup Calendars**

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066059139/original/DtaDFABwNTB99jODyi3on53c21Jqin-Z1g.jpeg?1772524422)

  

#### **Step 3:** Turn on Rentals (Multiple Sub-Accounts)

  

-   Use the toggle to Turn On **Rentals**  
      
    
-   Type **CONFIRM** then click the blue **Save** button to save your changes

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155066060522/original/xFm3Vd6powAWEkV5h9yJ13OJJxy7TJr8Ag.png?1772525378)

## **Getting Started with Rentals**

  

#### **Step 1:** Access Rentals

  
Go to **Calendars → Calendar Settings → Rentals**

  

**![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155056178317/original/XU-M2Nj6rWJb1ZhuJoe7nnGsPa216DWRnA.png?1760634200)**

  

  

#### **Step 2:** Create Listing

  

-   Click **\+ New Listing** to add your first rental item or property.  
      
    
-   Configure its name, description, price, and availability.  
      
    
-   Use **Advanced Settings** to define inventory, variants, pricing, and booking rules.  
      
    

Looking for more information on how to create a listing? [Click here to read our article on Creating and Editing Listings](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006579)

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155056178583/original/InaMf8rORf-wMVdUVjY7_SF0KWTBMGfl8w.png?1760634290)

  

  

#### **Step 3:** Customize Global Settings

  
Navigate to **Rentals →  Global Settings** to adjust business hours, communication settings, and payment methods.

  

Looking for more information on how customize global settings? [Click here to read our article on Global Settings in Rentals](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006640)

#### **Step 4:** Test a Booking

  
Create a test booking from the Calendar View or Appointments tab to verify configuration, pricing, and notifications.

## **Known Issues / Limitations**

  

As Rentals is currently in public beta, please be aware of the following:

  

-   Certain features are still a work in progress and will be made available soon.
-   You might occasionally experience minor performance or UI inconsistencies as we continue refining the tool.
-   **Payment summary refresh :** After recording a payment, the **Payment** Summary and **Invoices/Transactions** may take a moment to update. If totals don’t reflect immediately, **refresh the page** to see the latest state.  
      
    

We greatly appreciate your understanding and encourage you to share your feedback to help us quickly address these areas.

## **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

  

**Q: How can I check if Rentals is activated for any location?**  
A: Go to **Calendars → Calendar Settings** to see if the **Rentals** tab is visible.  
  

**Q: Will my data transfer to the general release?**  
A: Yes. All data created during the beta will remain available when Rentals moves out of beta.
