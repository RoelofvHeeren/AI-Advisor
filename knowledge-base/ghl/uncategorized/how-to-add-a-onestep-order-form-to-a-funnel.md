# How to Add a One‑Step Order Form to a Funnel

This article walks through adding and configuring a **One‑Step Order Form** in a HighLevel funnel. You’ll attach products and prices, configure fields (contact, shipping, payment) and enable options like Cart Mode, Order Bump, and Coupon Codes.

# **What is a One‑Step Order Form?**

  

One‑Step Order Form places all checkout fields, such as contact, optional shipping, product selection, and payment, on a single screen. It’s ideal for low‑friction offers, returning customers, and mobile‑first flows where fewer taps matter. Compared with a two‑step layout, it removes navigation but increases on‑page density so keep only essential fields and use clear labels. One‑step forms support one‑time and subscription prices.

* * *

## **Key Benefits of One‑Step Order Forms**

  

-   **Speed to Purchase**: Reduces clicks by consolidating checkout into one page.  
    
-   **All Details Visible**: Buyers can confirm products, totals, and payment at a glance.  
      
    
-   **Flexible Fields:** Customize the fields that are shown/hidden and required/optional to match your needs.  
      
    
-   **Cart Mode:** Cart mode and quantity limits let buyers select exactly what they need while keeping totals clear.  
      
    
-   **Revenue Boosters:** Order bump support makes it easy to offer complementary items on checkout.

## **Products Setup (Prerequisite)**

  

Products must be attached to the funnel step before the One‑Step Order Form can display and charge for them. Follow these steps to ensure your products and prices are available in the form.

  

**Tip:** Be sure to create your products before starting this process. To learn more about creating products, [click here.](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000005071)

  

1.  Go to **Sites** in the left navigation, open your **Funnel**, and select the **funnel step** you want to use.  
      
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168376/original/VPC01EApF6HRAM139B5--5HsueYzsji3lQ.png?1767823720)  
      
    
2.  Open the **Products** tab on the right. Click **\+ Add Product**.  
      
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168366/original/0AF7X5VVm51amU1D-B7Dfm_yXcABevoaNg.png?1767823687)  
      
    
3.  Use the **Product** dropdown to select the item you want to sell or click the inline text link to **create a new product**.  
      
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168403/original/hrG_WYHKPkY1yfPLJpFegY1JmjXr757UjA.png?1767823768)  
      
    
4.  Under **Price**, choose an existing price or click the inline text link to **create a new price**.  
      
    You can optionally use **Product Name Override** to edit the name to control how it appears on the order form or **Price Display Override** to customize how the price is shown on the order form.  
      
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168416/original/WZhPEh-Bi2dqpUYa4_elngkwrO2Avz5Mlg.png?1767823801)  
      
    
5.  Click **Additional options** to Allow multiple quantity, set Max per order and Mark this item as a Main product or a Bump product.  
      
    ![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168423/original/17wIIVb4Y7zbJylVNAuY5A-x83xSek3reg.png?1767823829)  
      
    
6.  Repeat to add one or many products as needed!

## **Adding the One‑Step Order Form to a Funnel**

  

After products are attached to the step, follow these steps to add and configure the One‑Step Order Form element in your funnel.

####   

#### **S****tep 1:** Navigate to your Funnel Step

  

Go to **Sites → Funnels**, open your funnel, and select the specific step where you’ll place the checkout.

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168448/original/EOC-EIh5lt9bja2XRvkkUF471C7GESPhYw.jpeg?1767823932)

  

  

#### **Step 2:** Add the One‑Step Order Form Element

  

Click **Add Elements** **(+)** in the top‑left and under **Order elements**, select the **One Step Order** element. Drag this element into your design.

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168480/original/35X6xZAEky5koXzMrvIA22dF0S8efUf56g.png?1767824046)

  

  

#### **Step 3:** Configure Fields

  

In the element settings, open **Form options**. Here you can configure all fields included in the form such as Company Name, Shipping and Price.

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168540/original/P7HFgQHKGuym7rpkVBO6r_3y41hmxsfRAA.png?1767824216)

  

  

#### **Step 4:** Other Settings (Optional) 

  

Use the additional settings to control how products are presented, whether you offer add‑ons, and if buyers can apply discounts:  
  

-   Toggle **Enable cart mode** (when available for your funnel version). To learn more about Shopping Cart Mode in Order Forms, [click here!](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/48001221440)  
      
    
-   Choose whether to show **Product description** and **Price**.  
      
    
-   Enable and configure an **Order Bump.** To learn more about Order Bumps in Forms, [click here!](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000006914)  
      
    
-   Enable **Coupon Codes.**  
    [](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000005071)  
    

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168556/original/3EstZ6u5rNrliMKWFn6WhuXw0zJdysqZXw.png?1767824262)

  

  

#### **Step 5:** Style the Form

  

In the element settings, adjust **Button, Text and Background** **Colors** to match your brand.

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168575/original/gg2AShKDWdLBe_LRiGsMY-r1t6OLYwvSdQ.png?1767824298)

  

  

#### **Step 6:** Preview and Publish

  

Before publishing your funnel, click the **Preview** button in the upper-right corner to test your order form. Once verified, click the blue **Publish** button.

  

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/cdn.freshdesk.com/data/helpdesk/attachments/production/155062168580/original/TkEk-CQwPG4xOV1Up24K1bBBJfalGj_6MA.png?1767824330)

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

  

**Q: Why don’t my products appear in the One‑Step Order Form?**

Make sure products and at least one price are added in the Products tab for the same funnel step.

  

**Q: How do I let customers buy multiple quantities?** 

In Products → Additional options, enable Allow multiple quantity and set Max per order.

  

**Q: Can I use a one‑step order form in V1 funnels?**

One‑step order forms can be added in funnels. Consider migrating the step to V2 to access the latest settings such as cart mode.

  

**Q: Does the order form support subscriptions and one‑time products?**

Yes. The order form reflects the Price you attach to the product (one‑time or recurring).

  

**Q: Can I add a platform/processing fee to all orders?**

Yes! Processing or platform fees can be added under payments settings. [Click here to learn more!](https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000005519)
